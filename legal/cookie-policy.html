Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) Lua Tools (franrivalbigprojects.github.io/lua-tools) uses, why we use them, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, which browsers use to store small pieces of data directly on your device without expiration dates.

Categories

  • Strictly necessary: local storage for theme and consent preference. These do not require consent and cannot be disabled through our banner, as the site would not remember your basic preferences without them.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (GA4) cookies, used to understand which tools are used and how the site performs. Requires your consent.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense cookies, used to serve and measure ads. Requires your consent.

Your choices

When you first visit Lua Tools, a banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies (analytics and advertising). You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, or through your browser's cookie settings. Declining does not affect your ability to use the Validator, Formatter, Minifier, or Pattern Tester — all core functionality runs entirely in your browser regardless of your cookie choice.
