Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lua Tools (franrivalbigprojects.github.io/lua-tools) handles information when you use the site.

How the tools work

The Validator, Formatter, Minifier, and Pattern Tester all run entirely in your browser. Any Lua code, patterns, or test strings you paste into the site are processed locally on your device and are never sent to, stored on, or seen by our servers. We have no visibility into the code you validate, format, minify, or test.

Information we collect

  • Usage analytics (Google Analytics, GA4): if you consent, we collect anonymized/aggregated data such as which tool tabs you open, whether you run a validation/format/minify/pattern-test action, and which related tools you click through to. This helps us understand which features are useful and where to improve. No code content is ever included in this data.
  • Rating submissions: if you rate the tool, we store the star rating you submit (via a lightweight backend) to display an aggregate score. No personal identifiers are attached to your rating.
  • Advertising data (Google AdSense): subject to your consent and once approved, Google may use cookies to serve and measure ads, and may use data as described in Google's own policies (linked below).
  • Theme preference: stored locally on your device only (light/dark mode), never transmitted to us.

What we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, login, or email address to use any tool.
  • We do not collect or store the Lua code, patterns, or strings you enter.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Legal basis and consent

Where required by applicable law (such as the EU/UK GDPR or similar regulations), we ask for your consent before enabling analytics or advertising cookies, via the consent banner shown on your first visit. You can decline non-essential cookies and still use all tools without restriction. See our Cookie Policy for details on individual cookies.

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services, which have their own privacy practices:

  • Google Analytics / Google AdSense — Google Privacy Policy

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, and to withdraw consent at any time. Since we do not require accounts and store minimal identifiable data, most requests can be resolved simply by clearing your browser's local storage and cookies for this site, or by declining non-essential cookies in the consent banner.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed via the contact information on the Lua Tools project page.
